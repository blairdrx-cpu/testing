Why Most Drummers Stay Stuck

You've Been Here Before.

You're still guessing whether your compression sounds right. You've watched the tutorials. You know you need more than information — you need someone to tell you if your mix is good.

Turning Knobs by Feel

Inconsistent results. Sometimes good, sometimes not. You don't know why, so you can't repeat the wins or fix the losses.

The YouTube Trap

Scattered tutorials that work in their room, with their gear. You have different mics, different acoustics, different problems. YouTube can't answer "what should I do in my specific situation?"

No One to Ask

You're working alone. No one in your life understands phase relationships, source sounds, or why your room sounds the way it does. You need people who get it — and someone who's been there.

Undercharging From Uncertainty

You're charging $75–150/track because you don't trust your mixes enough to ask for $200. That's not a pricing problem. It's a knowledge problem.

3-Phase Curriculum

01

Source Sounds & Recording

Weeks 1–6. Tuning, mic technique, phase relationships, playing for the mics. 80% of your sound happens before you press record.

02

Mixing & Refinement

Weeks 7–9. EQ, compression, effects, drum bus, editing, professional file delivery. Stop guessing what compression is doing.

03

Business & Client Systems

Weeks 10–12. Rate calculation, client communication, getting work, building momentum. Technical skills get you the first gig. Systems get you rehired.

How the Live Coaching Works

16 Weeks.
No Hiding.

Weekly Group Calls

  • 90-minute live sessions via Zoom every week for 16 weeks
  • Q&A on curriculum material, technical problems, business questions
  • Track reviews — submit your mixes, get feedback in front of the group
  • Real problems: "My snare sounds boxy — here's the clip"
  • Business questions: "Should I raise my rates now or wait?"
  • All calls recorded and posted with timestamps within 24 hours
  • Submit questions beforehand if you can't attend live — they get answered

Track Review Process

  • Submit tracks through the community platform
  • Blair reviews during the group call — everyone learns from it
  • Feedback on EQ choices, compression, effects, overall decisions
  • What's working, what needs adjustment — direct and specific
  • Follow-up submissions encouraged: "Here's my revision — better?"
  • Real mixes, real problems, real solutions — not theoretical examples
  • You often learn as much from other people's tracks as your own
