The Real Problem

Most drum recordings fail
before you press record.

You're not lazy about this. You watch the videos, you experiment, you try different things. But the recording still sounds off — and you can't put your finger on why. That's not a gear problem. It's a fundamentals problem.

You can hear that something's wrong, but you don't have the vocabulary or framework to diagnose it — so you keep adjusting things at random.

Your room is shaping the sound before the mic even picks it up. Most drummers never learn this — and it's the first thing that needs to be understood.

Mic placement looks simple but it isn't. A few centimeters changes everything. Without a system, you're guessing every session.

Tuning by feel means you can't recreate a sound or understand why it changed. A repeatable method fixes this immediately.

No. You need a drum kit and 2–4 mics. This course is built around the idea that your setup is not the problem. Your approach is. Expensive gear doesn't fix a bad foundation.

I've never recorded before. Is this too advanced?

This is specifically built for beginners. We start with a single mic and build from there. No prior recording knowledge required.

I'm not trying to get session work. Is this still useful?

Yes — that's exactly who this is for. Room & Kit has no business content. It's entirely focused on getting better sounds for your own recordings and projects.

What's the difference between this and the Self-Paced Course?
